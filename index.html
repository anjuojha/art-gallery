Meet the Artist

Anju Ojha

Based in Vile Parle, Mumbai, Anju Ojha is a fine artist specializing in original acrylic, oil, and mixed-media paintings. With a deep devotion to wildlife, fine portraiture, and natural compositions, her work celebrates the spirit and living energy of the natural world.

Each original piece is handcrafted using traditional materials and techniques. Guided by character, emotion, and personality rather than rigid perfection, Anju captures those subtle, endearing moments and expressions that pet owners and art lovers hold dear to their hearts.
